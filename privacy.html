Privacy Policy

How SkillRise Academy handles your information. Last updated: October 2026.

What information do we collect?
When you apply for admission or create an account, we collect your name, email address, and chosen course. The app also stores your login session and course progress on your own device.
How is my information used?
Only to provide our services: confirming your admission, activating course access, and responding to your support messages. We never sell your data.
Is my data shared with third parties?
No. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any third party, except where required by law.
Payments
Course fees are paid through manual card or bank transfer instructions shown at checkout. We do not store your card numbers — payment details go directly to your bank or card provider.
Data stored on your device
The app may cache course pages, images, and videos on your device so lessons load faster and work offline. You can clear this anytime in your device settings.
Children
SkillRise Academy courses are designed for learners aged 13 and above. Accounts for younger learners should be created and supervised by a parent or guardian.
Contact us
For any privacy question, reach us through the contact page — we reply within 24 hours.
